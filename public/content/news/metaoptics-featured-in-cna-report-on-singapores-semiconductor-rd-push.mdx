  <div className="mdx-text">
    MetaOptics has been featured in a CNA report on Singapore's semiconductor ambitions. Over the next five years, semiconductor industry players will spend S$500 million on research and development projects in Singapore, up from the S$400 million spent between 2020 and 2025. Today, one in every ten chips worldwide is made in Singapore, a position built on years of investment in research and in taking technology from the lab to real products.
  </div>

  <div className="mdx-text">
    As AI demands more from chips, the traditional way of making them faster, by adding more transistors, is reaching its limits. Singapore has been working on alternatives for over a decade, including advanced packaging, heterogeneous integration, and using light instead of electricity to move data more efficiently.
  </div>

  <div className="mdx-text">
    The report features MetaOptics as an example of how ASTAR supports small and medium enterprises in the sector. That support came in the form of talent, intellectual property and R&amp;D collaboration, including crucial help in developing the machines used to make flat optics. These can be used in sensors such as cameras and in high speed data connections. Since then, MetaOptics has gone public and its products have reached overseas markets, progress the company partly credits to ASTAR's support. Access to shared infrastructure has also played a role, as building a dedicated cleanroom alone would have been a significant cost.
  </div>

  <div className="mdx-text">
    Looking ahead, Singapore plans to focus its next five year national research plan on helping companies turn promising technology into sustainable businesses.
