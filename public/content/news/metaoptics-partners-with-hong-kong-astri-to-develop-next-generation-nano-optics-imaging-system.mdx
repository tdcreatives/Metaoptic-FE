  <div className="mdx-text">
    MetaOptics Partners with Hong Kong's ASTRI to Develop a Next-Generation Nano-Optics Imaging System
  </div>

  <div className="mdx-text">
    SINGAPORE, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- <b>MetaOptics Ltd (Catalist: 9MT) (OTCQX: MOTLY) ("MetaOptics" or the "Company", and together with its subsidiaries, the "Group")</b>, a leading-edge semiconductor optics company, today announced that, through its wholly-owned subsidiary MetaOptics Technologies Pte. Ltd., it will collaborate with the Hong Kong Applied Science and Technology Research Institute ("ASTRI") to jointly develop a compact nano-optics imaging system for advanced biometric security and smart sensing applications, with sponsorship support from MetaOptics.
  </div>

  <h3 className="font-bold">Combining Metalens and Metasurface Technologies with Edge AI</h3>

  <div className="mdx-text">
    The system will pair MetaOptics' imaging metalens technology with ASTRI's polarimetric metasurface design and edge-AI biometric algorithms. By capturing both visible-light and near-infrared polarisation information in one compact system, it is expected to offer richer sensing and stronger anti-spoofing for high-security authentication.
  </div>

  <div className="mdx-text">
    MetaOptics' proprietary Direct Laser Writer ("DLW") platform will support rapid prototyping of the optical components, enabling faster design-test-refine cycles. There are also interests in exploring the DLW platform's potential for wearable devices, automotive sensing, and AIoT applications.
  </div>

  <h3 className="font-bold">Strengthening MetaOptics' Presence in Hong Kong and the Greater Bay Area</h3>

  <div className="mdx-text">
    As part of the collaboration, MetaOptics will place its DLW platform at ASTRI's facilities at the Hong Kong Science Park. It will be used for the joint project alongside ASTRI's cleanroom and nanofabrication capabilities.
  </div>

  <div className="mdx-text">
    The collaboration extends MetaOptics' footprint in Hong Kong, an international innovation and technology hub. It also supports closer engagement with customers and partners across the Greater Bay Area.
  </div>

  <div className="mdx-text">
    "Our collaboration with ASTRI extends MetaOptics into high-value imaging applications such as biometric security, and brings our metalens technology closer to customers in Hong Kong and the Greater Bay Area," said Mr Thng Chong Kim, Executive Chairman of MetaOptics. "Working with ASTRI, with our DLW platform on-site, will help us accelerate development and engage customers earlier. We believe this partnership broadens our customer pipeline and supports long-term value creation for our shareholders."
  </div>

  <div className="mdx-text">
    "ASTRI is committed to translating cutting-edge optoelectronics and AI research into commercial solutions for industry partners across Hong Kong, Chinese Mainland and beyond," said Dr. Ying Huang, Chief Technology Officer, ASTRI. "By combining ASTRI's expertise in polarisation metasurface design, nanofabrication and edge-AI biometric recognition with MetaOptics' metalens and DLW technology, we can shorten the R&amp;D cycle and bring compact, high-performance sensing systems to market faster."
  </div>
